Sloth Jam for VTubers and streamers.

Sloth Jam is a cozy family party game with 64 one-minute mini-games, kart racing and an island to explore, played with one stick and one button and no reading needed. We team up with VTubers to bring their persona into the game, and launch it together on stream so new players can find the island.

Your persona stays yours

  • You keep full ownership of your persona and characters. Sloth Jam only gets non-exclusive permission to use them in the game and in announcements about the collaboration.
  • You approve everything. We share work in progress at each step, and nothing goes into the game or gets announced without your OK.
  • You can say no to anything, or end the collaboration any time by email, and your character is removed in the next update if you ask.

The deal

This is an unpaid creative collaboration and cross-promotion, not a sponsorship. Nobody owes anybody money. We build your character, and in return we ask for one thing: a launch stream. We work with a small group of creators at a time.

What we ask

  • One launch stream or video. When your character goes live, play Sloth Jam on stream or post one video of it. We pick the launch day together, around your schedule.
  • Link us. Put slothjam.com in your stream or video description.
  • Say it's a collab. Your character is a gift from us, so let viewers know, for example with #ad or your platform's collab label, the way your platform and local rules ask.
  • That's all. More streams, clips and the event week are welcome and always up to you.

How it works

  1. 1

    Apply with the form below.

    We chat about your persona and ideas.

  3. 3

    You send references or designs, we build your character and share work in progress.

  4. 4

    You approve it, it goes live, and you play it on your launch stream while we announce it together.

Streaming rules

For anyone streaming Sloth Jam.

  • Stream it freely. You're free to stream, clip, share VODs and highlights, and monetize your videos, no need to ask. The soundtrack is cleared for streaming.
  • Keep streams family friendly.
  • Link us. Please link slothjam.com in your description.
  • Play locally. Keep online play off on stream and play locally. Online play is off by default. If it's ever on, don't show or call out other players' sloths, since some of them may be kids.
