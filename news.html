About the game

Short

Sloth Jam is a cozy party game the whole family can play: 64 one-minute party games on a sloth island, played with one stick and one button, with no reading needed. Free in your web browser.

Long

Sloth Jam is a cozy party game for ages 6 and up, free to play in any web browser and as an app for Android devices and Amazon Fire tablets. It has 64 party games, and every one lasts one minute, from Coconut Dodge to Pond Hockey. A match is three rounds with the last one counting double. Between games, players explore an island that keeps growing: dive a coral reef, ride a chairlift up Snowcap Peak, hop in a toy rocket to the Moon, fish, garden, cook and dress up. A story told only in picture cards, the Great Jam, has players put the town back together after a storm. Chat only sends kind words from a short list, a grown-up makes the family account, and there are no loot boxes and no pay to win. Sloth Jam is designed and built by Remi Couture, a children's game designer who started on Club Penguin.
