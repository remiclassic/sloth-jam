SHORT DESCRIPTION
Sloth Jam is a cozy party game the whole family can play: 56 one-minute party games on a sloth island, played with one stick and one button, with no reading needed. Free in your web browser.

LONG DESCRIPTION
Sloth Jam is a cozy party game for ages 6 and up, free to play in any web browser. Every party game lasts one minute, from Coconut Dodge to Pond Hockey, and a match is three rounds with the last one counting double. Between games, players explore an island that keeps growing: dive a coral reef, ride a chairlift up Snowcap Peak, hop in a toy rocket to the Moon, fish, garden, cook and dress up. A story told only in picture cards, the Great Jam, has players put the town back together after a storm. Chat only sends kind words from a short list, a grown-up makes the family account, and there are no loot boxes and no pay to win. Sloth Jam is designed and built by Remi Couture, a children's game designer who started on Club Penguin.
