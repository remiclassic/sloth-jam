Sloth Jam for Schools

Sloth Jam is free for your classroom. No time limit, no catch.

A family party game with 64 mini-games, each played with one stick and one button. Made for ages 6+, and kids can play without reading.

Play now

Open slothjam.com in any browser and start playing. No student accounts needed.

Why it works in a classroom

  • No reading needed

    Kids can jump in and play, including early readers and students still learning English or French.

  • Simple controls

    Every mini-game uses one stick and one button. Nobody needs a tutorial.

  • Group play

    It is a party game, built for playing together and cheering each other on.

  • Short rounds

    Mini-games fit easily into a reward break, indoor recess or the last few minutes before the bell.

  • No loot boxes

How to play in class

  • On a projector or smart board

    Open slothjam.com in a browser and play as a whole class.

  • On tablets

    Open slothjam.com in the browser, or install it from Google Play or Amazon.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Sloth Jam is free for schools, classrooms and families, with no time limit and no catch.

What ages is it for?

Sloth Jam is made for ages 6+.

Do students need to read to play?

No. Kids can play without reading.

What do we need to play in class?

A browser on a computer connected to a projector or smart board, or tablets. Sloth Jam runs on the web at slothjam.com and is also on Google Play and Amazon. No student accounts needed.

Are there loot boxes or in-game purchases?

Sloth Jam is free to play. The only purchase is an optional Founder Pack, and there are no loot boxes.

How do you handle student privacy?

Sloth Jam collects no personal data from players, and no student accounts are needed. Our privacy policy is at slothjam.com/privacy.html.

Who makes Sloth Jam?

Strategic Sloth, a studio founded by Remi Couture, a former Club Penguin artist.
