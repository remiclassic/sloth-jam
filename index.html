Island friends

Same island, more friends.

Ten other animals live around the edge of town, from an otter to a yeti. Walk up and they wave, answer your emotes and chat, and at night they curl up and sleep.

The party games

Every game lasts one minute.

A match is three rounds, and the last one counts double. Last place picks the game for the final, and 49 party twists like Fruit Storm and Double Gold change a game for one round. Computer players play like a kind sibling, tuned in our tests so a new player wins about one round in four.

Fruit Fall

Grab a basket and catch the good fruit as it drops. The lumpy, frowning ones are rotten. Leave those.

Sloth Tag

Whoever is "it" wears the crown. Dash, dodge, and stay inside the ring while it shrinks.

Pop the Moles

Nine holes, one foam mallet. Build a combo, watch for the golden mole, and mind the hedgehog.

Lantern Parade

Nobody loses this one. Light every lantern together before the time runs out and share the stars.

Hammock Hop

A ring of swinging hammocks and a long way down. The last sloth standing wins.

Crystal Cart

Ride a mine cart through the crystal cave. Lean into the bends, hop the gaps, spin for trick points.

Plus Snack Stash, Bunny Roundup, Stone Skip, Hat Whack, Coconut Dodge, Vine Tug, Pond Hockey and 51 more.

New on the game shelf

Pond Hockey

Two teams on Snowcap's frozen pond, with a goalie in each net. Tap for a shot, hold for a big slapshot.

Coconut Dodge

Coconuts roll down a sandy lane. Sidestep the big ones and hop over the small ones.

Hat Whack

Swing at the other sloths' hats and keep yours on your head. A lost hat soon comes back.

Star Skate

Glide over the stars in order to draw shapes on the ice.

Snowball Splat

Throw snowballs at the snowmen in the middle of a snowy ring.

Pair Up

A calm tap puzzle: twelve face-down cards, six pairs to find.

  • Team rounds

    Some rounds split the party into two teams that share the score.

  • Party twists

    Most games have a twist that changes them for one round, like a fruit storm or a golden mole that shows up twice as often.

  • Walk up and play

    Every game has a signpost in the town, so you can play just that one.

Sloth Kart

Five tracks. Everybody races.

A free-for-all kart race. Push the stick the way you want to go, hold the button through a bend to drift, and drive through an item box for a pepper, a banana, a shell, a seeker or a star. Gates, laps, coins and your finish all score, so every racer takes points home.

Plaza Loop

Wide and gentle, round the town. Three laps, and the one to learn on.

Coconut Coast

S-bends along the beach, with palms and rock pools in the road.

Frosty Peak

A hairpin and slippery ice all the way round. Mind the snowmen.

Lava Run

A narrow road over the lava, with tight turns and no rails. A kart that drops off is put back on the road.

Rainbow Sky

Sweeping curves over the clouds and boost pads all the way. The best one to drift on.

  • Drift for a boost

    Hold the button through a bend until the sparks turn blue, then orange, then pink. Let go to shoot ahead.

  • Help at the back

    Karts at the back get the better items, so nobody is left alone at the end of the pack.

  • Five flags

    Each track has its own flag behind the go-kart pad in town, so you can race just that one.

Snowcap Peak

A snowy mountain across the water.

Find the telescope on the north shore and look through it to go there. Ride the chairlift up, then come down on a snowboard, skis or a sled.

Seven winter games live up here: Powder Run, Ski Slalom, Ski Jump, Snowball Splat, Slippery Stones, Star Skate and Pond Hockey.

Clover Vale

A pony valley through the telescope.

Turn the telescope to Clover Vale and step into a green valley of rivers, bridges and caves. Climb on the pony at the hitching rail and ride: a gentle push walks, all the way gallops, and the jump button clears logs and fences. Twelve golden horseshoes are hidden around the valley.

Pony rides

Walk, trot or gallop down the paths. Nothing can hurt the pony: it stops softly at a tree, a cliff or deep water.

Pony Joust

Ride at each other down the lists and lower your lance at just the right moment. Nobody falls off.

Hoof Hurdles

Gallop side by side down a show-jumping run and jump each fence as it comes.

Ring Tilt

Steer your lance tip through the rings over your lane. Everyone gets the very same rings.

The Great Jam

A story with no words.

A big wind has blown the festival away. The bunting is down, the fountain is dry and a bridge has lost its planks. Help the Sloth Family put the town back together, and watch the town change as you go.

  • Told in pictures

    Picture cards and thought bubbles carry the whole story. Nobody has to read.

  • No score to beat

    No quest asks for a score. Finishing a round is enough.

  • Nine chapters in

    All nine chapters are in the game: meet the Grumbles, earn tools like a toy sword and a lantern, and bring three Wind Chimes home. A story book lets you replay any part.

New places

The island keeps growing.

Places to explore at your own pace. Nothing out there can hurt your sloth.

Coral Cove

Dive off the jetty to a reef under the sea. Meet eight sea friends, find six pearls in giant clams, and open the chest in the wreck.

The Moon

Climb into the toy rocket on the beach and blast off. Bounce through the craters in a glass helmet, with the Earth hanging in the sky.

Skate Isle

A whole island that is one big skatepark. Pick a skateboard, scooter, bike or roller skates, then hop for tricks in the half-pipe and the bowl.

Pinecone Ridge

A green summer mountain with four bike trails, from a lazy meadow way to a jump line. Each trail has a clock, if you want one.

Sky Isle

Step on the sky lift pad on the west lawn and rise to an island in the clouds. Ride the wind spouts, glide through ten rings under a leaf and find ten golden feathers. Fall off the edge and you float back.

Clockwork Workshop

A steampunk shop with a giant top hat on the roof and an airship circling it. Professor Sprocket, the island’s inventor, keeps the counter, and some party games are played right in the room.

  • The Sleepy Sandcastle

    A gentle dungeon on the beach: torches, keys and crates, and a giant turtle to wake.

  • Shrines

    Four little puzzle rooms around town, each solved with the stick alone. None can be failed.

  • Vine rings

    Glowing rings you swing up to on a vine, no aiming needed, to reach cliff tops and tree crowns.

  • Warp stones and an owl

    Wake eight standing stones to hop around the island, and follow a friendly owl to places not found yet.

Home and play

A house, a stall and a lot of toys.

My house

Your own house on the lawn, with 26 pieces of furniture to place and paints for the walls. Earn a bigger room with levels and coins.

Your stall

Make things at the bench and sell them at your stall. You pick a price tag, and nobody can overcharge.

Go-kart

Hop in and drive the ring road. Take all eight stars that hang over it for a lap.

Hot air balloon

Float up over the whole island, with family sloths running over to ride along.

  • Cooking

  • Tall grass and berries

    Run through tall grass or shake a berry bush and small surprises pop out: a coin, a sprout, a butterfly.

  • DJ Ana

The sloths

Dress-up play

Put on the whole chef, pirate or wizard outfit and the town plays along. 16 roles, with no score and no way to lose.

The Dress Maker

Questions

Quick answers.

What is Sloth Jam?

Sloth Jam is a free cozy party game for the whole family that you play in a web browser. It has 64 one-minute party games on a sloth island, a kart race with five tracks, a story told in pictures, and slow, cozy things to do like fishing, gardening, cooking and dress-up. It is made by Strategic Sloth LLC.

Is Sloth Jam free?

Yes. Sloth Jam is free to play in your web browser while it is in early access, which means it is still being built and has rough edges. Founder packs are optional and only give digital, cosmetic rewards. Nothing in the album needs one, and there is no pay to win.

What ages is Sloth Jam for?

Sloth Jam is made for ages 6 and up. No reading is needed: play is pictures and numbers, with one stick and one button. Only the grown-up screens use words, so parents and kids can play the same game.

What do I need to play Sloth Jam?

A computer, phone or tablet with a web browser. There is nothing to download or install. Open https://slothjam.com/play/ and a grown-up signs in with their email before the first game. On a phone or tablet you can add it to your home screen to play full screen, like an app.

Can the whole family play Sloth Jam together?

Yes. In the Family Cup you pass one device around the sofa and play for the cup. Everyone gets their own sloth, and family profiles share one closet of outfits. Online play with other families is in early testing and stays off until a grown-up turns it on behind the grown-ups lock. Friends then join by a picture code, never by searching.

Is Sloth Jam safe for my child?

Yes. Chat is a short list of kind words, so no names, ages or numbers get through. A grown-up makes the family account with a code sent by email, and a child never signs up. There are no loot boxes, no streaks that break, no limited-time offers, and a parent gate guards every money screen.

Is Sloth Jam like Club Penguin?

Sloth Jam is a cozy island game for families, made by Remi Couture, who was an illustrator at Disney on Club Penguin from 2006 to 2009. You can play party games, dress up your sloth, decorate your own house and explore the island. Unlike Club Penguin, online play is off until a grown-up turns it on, and even then chat is only kind words from a short list. Sloth Jam is an independent game and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Disney.

Who makes Sloth Jam?

Sloth Jam is designed and built by Remi Couture, working as Strategic Sloth LLC. Remi has about twenty years of making games, many for children, including children's apps for DreamWorks and BBC Kids at Cupcake Digital.

What does a founder pack get me?

Digital, cosmetic rewards like a founder badge, outfits, the Founder House on the island and your name in the credits. Your money pays for playtesting, the Android app and new temples and side quests for the story.

How do I get my founder code?

It arrives by email a minute or two after you buy. In the game, open the grown-up menu, choose Redeem a backer code and paste it in.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Reply to your receipt email within 30 days for a full refund.
