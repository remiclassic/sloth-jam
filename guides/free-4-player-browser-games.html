Free 4 player games in the browser

A parent guide · October 2026

You can play together with four people on one device. On a TV, pass a controller or a small gamepad, on a tablet or laptop, sit close and take turns. The tips below keep the pace quick and the sharing kind, with short party games and a kart mode that lets everyone race for points.

Set up one device for four

  • Use the biggest screen you have. A TV is best, a tablet or laptop works well too. Use a cable or casting as needed.
  • Seat order matters. Left to right works. Say who is first. For young kids, let last place choose the final game.
  • Keep rounds short. One minute games mean nobody waits long. Three rounds make a clean match with a natural end.

Pick four player friendly modes

  • Party games by turns. Everyone plays the same one minute game when it is their turn, then pass the device.
  • Table games and quick duels. Short two player bouts are great between full rounds. Swap pairs to mix it up.
  • Kart for everyone. A race where points come from laps, gates and coins gives every driver a reason to try. Sloth Jam has a full Sloth Kart mode where all racers earn points and a drift gives a boost when you let go.

Controls that work for a crowd

  • One stick and one button. With mixed ages, this keeps the focus on the fun. No long combos to remember.
  • Color code players. Use colors or an on screen label so it is always clear who is who.
  • Use a visible order. A simple order on paper or a small name list on screen helps kids know when they go.

Browser setup checklist

  • Open the game at slothjam.com and press play. It runs in the browser and is free while in early access.
  • Full screen the tab and turn on do not disturb so nothing pops up during a match.
  • Plug in a charger if you play on a phone or tablet. A low battery ends the fun early.
