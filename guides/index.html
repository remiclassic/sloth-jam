Family game guides

Practical ideas for parents · October 2026

These guides help families pick games for a birthday party, a rainy afternoon or a TV night. Each guide has small, useful steps you can try right away.   • Party games for a kid’s birthday party

    Simple stations, short rounds and clear turn taking keep the energy happy. Ideas with and without screens.

  • Family game night games on the TV

    Ways to get games on the big screen, from casting to a cable. Couch friendly games that work well with a remote or controller.

  • Games for kids who can’t read yet (ages 5–6)

    Pick games that teach by watching and use symbols. Keep waiting short and show progress without words.

  • Free 4 player games in the browser

    Gather around one device and play for a few minutes at a time. Good four player ideas, no installs.

  • Games grandparents and kids can play together

    Slow the pace, avoid tiny text, and pick rounds short enough for little legs. Options for gentle competition and full cooperation.

  • Rainy day indoor games for kids

    Mix movement with quiet play, with a short break every 15 minutes. Ideas for small spaces and mixed ages.
