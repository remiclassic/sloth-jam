Sloth Jam now has 64 one-minute party games

October 23, 2026 · Strategic Sloth

Sloth Jam now has 64 party games, and every one of them lasts one minute. They all play with one stick and one button, and no reading is needed, so a 6 year old and a grandparent can play the same game.

A match is three rounds, and the last round counts double. The games run from Coconut Dodge to Pond Hockey, with Hat Whack, Pop the Moles and Crystal Cart along the way.

Beyond the party games there is Sloth Kart, a kart race with five tracks, and the Great Jam, a story told in pictures.

Every game is free to play at slothjam.com/play, in a web browser on a computer, phone or tablet.
