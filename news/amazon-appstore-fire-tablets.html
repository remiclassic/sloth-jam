Sloth Jam is on the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

October 22, 2026 · Strategic Sloth

Families with an Amazon Fire tablet can now get Sloth Jam from the Amazon Appstore: Sloth Jam on Amazon.

It is the same cozy party game as on the web, made for ages 6 and up, with no reading needed, no loot boxes and no pay to win.

Not on a Fire tablet? Sloth Jam plays free in any web browser at slothjam.com/play.
