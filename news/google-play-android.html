Sloth Jam is now on Google Play for Android

October 23, 2026 · Strategic Sloth

Sloth Jam is now on Google Play, so Android devices can install it right from the store: Sloth Jam on Google Play.

The app opens the same game that plays at slothjam.com/play, so it gets new games and fixes as soon as they reach the website.

Like the browser version, it is free to play, made for ages 6 and up, and has no loot boxes and no pay to win. A grown-up signs in once with their email before the first game.

No Android device? Sloth Jam still plays free in any web browser, and Amazon Fire tablets can get it from the Amazon Appstore.
