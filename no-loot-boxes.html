No loot boxes

Fair rewards for kids · October 2026

Parents often ask if a game has loot boxes or pay to win. Sloth Jam does not. Here is how rewards work and how we keep money out of play.

No random paid rewards

Loot boxes and paid random drops teach the wrong lesson. Sloth Jam has none. Outfit pieces are earned by playing or bought for coins at a shown price, not at random. No loot boxes is one of our promises.

Never pay to win

Founder packs are optional and only give digital, cosmetic rewards like outfits and a house on the island. Nothing in the album needs one, and there is no pay to win. Party games are fair for everyone.

A parent gate on money screens

Any screen that deals with real money or redeem codes sits behind a simple parent gate. A child cannot open it by accident.

Ads stay out of the game

The game has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Kids play without interruptions. (This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and asks visitors from Europe first.)
